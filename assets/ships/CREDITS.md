# Ship artwork credits

The enemy ships in Type Blaster are renders of 3D models from the **Space Kit (2.0)** by **Kenney** (https://www.kenney.nl).

- Models used: `craft_speederA`, `craft_speederB`, `craft_speederC`, `craft_speederD`, `craft_racer`, `craft_miner`, `craft_cargoA`, `craft_cargoB`.
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Free for personal, educational and **commercial** use, no attribution required (we credit Kenney anyway, thank you!). Original license text: `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`.
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit  (support Kenney: https://kenney.nl/support)

`ships.webp` is a sprite sheet made by rendering those models with three.js (MIT license): 8 rows (one per ship), 13 columns (frames of a gentle banking sway), 144x144 px per frame, nose pointing down. The rendered images are derivative works of CC0 material.
